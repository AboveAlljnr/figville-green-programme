One programme, five ways to put solar to work.

Education creates demand. Community projects prove it works. Events and business development turn interest into installations.

  • Public education & media

    TV and radio discussions, social media, expert interviews and explainers on renewable energy and energy efficiency.

  • Community renewable projects

    Solar for selected underserved communities, schools, health facilities and NGOs.

  • Corporate sustainability & ESG

    Forums and sessions for businesses on the financial side of the clean-energy transition.

  • Events & activations

    Solar demonstrations, outreach days and stakeholder meetings that let people see the technology first-hand.

  • Renewable energy business development

    Households, SMEs and larger organisations are connected with Figville quotations and suitable financing options.

Who the programme reaches

Different people, different reasons to switch — but one programme that serves them all.

Households Affordable, reliable energy at home.

Institutions & NGOs Visible delivery on sustainability goals through solar projects and partnerships.
Government & development stakeholders Collaboration around clean-energy initiatives.

From agreement to impact — here's how it works.

The programme is structured, sequenced and fully reported at every stage.

  1. 1

    We agree the programme

    Terms, naming rights, branding and regulatory sign-off are finalised before anything launches.

  2. 2

    We launch nationally

    Media campaign, community events, and awareness activations kick off across Ghana.

  3. 3

    We install & connect

    Community solar projects are installed. Financing pathways for households and businesses are opened.

  4. 4

    We report impact

    A quarterly dashboard and annual verified impact report delivered to the naming partner and stakeholders.

A 12-month rollout, in four quarters.

The programme is agreed first, then delivered quarter by quarter.

  1. Pre-launch

    Agreements signed. Naming and branding approved with regulatory stakeholders.

  2. Quarter 1

    National launch and awareness campaign. Baseline measurements taken.

  3. Quarter 2

    Public education, community activations and lead capture.

  4. Quarter 3

    Projects installed. Financing pathways opened. Stories collected.

  5. Quarter 4

    Scale-up, impact report and renewal plan.

About Figville

Figville Company Limited is a Ghanaian renewable-energy company. We design, supply, install and maintain solar systems for households, commercial buildings, manufacturing plants, schools, hospitals and government facilities.

Our aim is simple: dependable clean power, delivered cleanly, affordably and reliably.
